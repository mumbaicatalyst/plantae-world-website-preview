The Plantae Standard

Why Turnkey Execution Matters

Landscaping in coastal India requires deep botanical science, civil precision, and unified on-site craft. We combine every discipline under a single dedicated studio.

Single-Point Accountability

Zero friction between design and civil build. Your landscape architect, horticulturalist, and site foreman operate as one unified team under a single fixed-scope contract.

Acclimatized Nursery Stock

Every specimen tree and tropical shrub is nurtured and acclimatized at our private nursery facility, specifically conditioned for Mumbai’s salt air, wind, and monsoon rhythms.

Engineered Micro-Irrigation

Automated, weather-smart drip systems calibrated to root depths and microclimates. Prevents slab waterlogging, eliminates runoff, and conserves water effortlessly.

Botanical Health Warranty

We stand behind the living health of every project with complimentary post-handover establishment care and tailored Annual Maintenance Contracts (AMC).
