Grown from
deep roots.

Plantae World carries forward a heritage that began in Mumbai in 1983 with Parasnath Enterprises. What started as a dedicated horticultural enterprise became a private wholesale nursery in 1985, establishing the botanical knowledge and material discipline that still grounds the studio today.

Also established in 1985, Plantae brought that living expertise into landscape architecture and urban consultation. Today, every commission is approached as a complete environment: shaped by climate, architecture, proportion, and the life that will unfold within it.

Owner & Lead Designer

Shruti Sanghvi brings a designer’s eye, grounded in horticulture.

Her work begins with a simple belief: a landscape should suit its place—its light, materials, seasons, and the way it is used.
