Grown from
deep roots.

Plantae World carries forward an unbroken horticultural legacy that began in Mumbai in 1983 with Parasnath Enterprises. What started as a dedicated plant enterprise became an esteemed private wholesale nursery, establishing the living knowledge and material discipline that grounds our entire practice today.

In 2019, that foundational expertise evolved into Plantae World, expanding across major Indian metros as a contemporary landscape practice. Today, as Plantae World LLP, we integrate architectural masterplanning, in-house nursery cultivation, civil execution, and lifetime horticultural stewardship into one seamless practice.

We do not treat a garden as decorative after-thought. We shape enduring, living environments tailored to climate, architecture, and the human lives that unfold within them.

The Plantae Approach

Six questions that shape
the landscape.

Before we recommend a single plant, we seek to understand the site and the people who will inhabit it.

01 / HOW WILL YOU USE IT?

Everyday Life

Everyday living, weekends, entertaining, children, and pets shape how the space works and moves.

02 / WHAT DOES THE SITE NEED?

Micro-Climate

Sunlight patterns, salt winds, shade, soil depth, and drainage dictate which botanical species truly flourish.

03 / HOW SHOULD IT FEEL?

Atmosphere

Privacy, open sky, serenity, vibrant energy, or a quiet retreat—curated planting defines character.

04 / WHAT NEEDS TO LAST?

Durability

Material resilience, mature root environments, automated irrigation, and practical maintenance planned together.

05 / CARE WHEN AWAY?

Reliable Systems

Concealed automated irrigation and seasonal AMC care protect second homes and managed properties.

06 / ARCHITECTURE DIALOGUE

Proportion

Sightlines, transitions, natural thresholds, and framing create an effortless continuum with the building.

Engineering For Growth

Designed for the Years Ahead

A considered landscape must grow more meaningful as it matures, not become another thing to manage.

  • Climate-Responsive Planting Plant palettes selected specifically for coastal maritime salt winds, high-rise heat reflections, and intense Indian monsoons.
  • Water-Conscious Selection & Smart Irrigation Multi-zone concealed drip irrigation and soil moisture calibration engineered to conserve municipal water.
  • Healthy Soils, Drainage & Root Environments Custom soil-free blends, organic microbial inputs, root aeration barriers, and high-flow sub-surface drainage membranes.
  • Durable, Maintainable Planting Strategies Growth projection modeling and scheduled stewardship so architectural spaces stay balanced as plantings reach maturity.
