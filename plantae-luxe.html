Studio Wisdom

The Luxe Care Ritual.

Every plant from Plantae Luxe comes with a tailored care schedule and ongoing WhatsApp guidance from our horticulturists.

Ruffled Fan Palm

Architectural Fan Foliage
Water: Every 7–10 days
Check: When top 1–2 inches feel dry
Avoid: Waterlogged root bases
Expert Tip: Thrives in bright indirect morning light.

Calathea

Living Prayer Leaf
Water: Every 5–7 days
Check: Keep soil lightly moist, never soggy
Avoid: Complete soil drought
Expert Tip: Loves humidity; mist leaves twice weekly.

ZZ Plant

Indestructible Gloss
Water: Every 2–3 weeks
Check: Let soil dry completely
Avoid: Overwatering & standing water
Expert Tip: Rhizomes store water for long dry spells.

Dracaena 'Dara Singh'

Stately Cane Specimen
Water: Every 10–14 days
Check: Allow top 2–3 inches to dry out
Avoid: Frequent shallow waterings
Expert Tip: Far more tolerant of underwatering than overwatering.
