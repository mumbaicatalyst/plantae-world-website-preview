Why Plantae World

Four Decades of Botanical Heritage & Design Mastery.

Founded in Mumbai in 1985 out of deep wholesale nursery roots, Plantae World brings 40+ years of botanical intelligence together with modern landscape architecture. Led by Principal Designer Shruti Sanghvi, we treat every commission as an enduring living system.

Unlike conventional landscape firms that outsource planting, our private wholesale nursery backbone allows us to acclimatize rare specimen trees and foliage specifically for coastal salt air, heavy monsoons, and urban microclimates before installation.
